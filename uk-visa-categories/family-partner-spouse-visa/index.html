Overview & Eligibility

The Partner/Spouse route under Appendix FM allows individuals to join their British citizen, settled, or qualifying partner in the UK. Applicants must demonstrate a genuine and subsisting relationship, meet the strict Minimum Income Requirement (MIR), satisfy the English language requirement, and have adequate accommodation that is not overcrowded.

Important: Financial requirement rules under Appendix FM-SE are rigid. Even a single missing payslip, omitted bank statement, or minor date discrepancy can cause an immediate refusal.

How We Prepare the Case

  1. 1Minimum Income Requirement (MIR) strategy and Category A/B/C/D/E financial reconciliation
  2. 2Evidence bundle assembly for genuine and subsisting relationship
  3. 3Accommodation inspection report and land registry review
  4. 4English language SELT certificate verification (CEFR Level A1)
  5. 5Online application filing, IHS settlement fee, and VFS submission

Key Eligibility Checklist

  • Partner is a British citizen, settled in the UK (ILR/PR), or holds qualifying status
  • Both parties are aged 18 or over at application date
  • Legally recognized marriage or civil partnership, or 2+ years cohabitation
  • Meets the Minimum Income Requirement or cash savings threshold
  • Adequate accommodation available without recourse to public funds
  • English language proficiency at CEFR A1 (A2/B1 for extensions)

Documents You May Need

  • Valid passport of applicant and copy of partner's British passport or BRP
  • Marriage certificate (Nikahnama) with certified English translation and NADRA registration
  • 6 to 12 months payslips, matching bank statements, and employer letter from UK partner
  • P60 / HMRC tax documents for self-employed sponsors
  • Tenancy agreement, Land Registry title deed, and property inspection report
  • Call logs, chat records, wedding photos, and travel tickets proving subsisting relationship
  • TB medical test clearance certificate from IOM Pakistan

A Word of Caution

Protect Yourself — Say No to Illegal Immigration

Every year, applicants lose their savings, and sometimes their lives, to unlicensed agents who promise a guaranteed UK visa or an illegal route to Britain. There is no shortcut into the UK. No consultancy — including ours — can promise a visa outcome.

Official Home Office Compliance Policy
  • Never attempt to enter or remain in the UK through unofficial, undocumented or "donkey" routes — these are dangerous, illegal, and can result in a lifetime UK entry ban.
  • Be wary of any agent who guarantees a visa, offers to bypass the interview or document process, or asks for large sums of undocumented cash "under the table."
  • Never submit fabricated documents, fake job offers, forged bank statements or bogus sponsorships — this can lead to a permanent ban and criminal liability in both Pakistan and the UK.
  • Always verify a consultancy's credentials and deal only in written, receipted agreements — never hand over your original passport or documents without a signed record.
  • Cross-check any requirement, fee or process you are told against the official Home Office source at gov.uk before paying anyone.
Verify Official Guidance:Visit GOV.UK Direct
